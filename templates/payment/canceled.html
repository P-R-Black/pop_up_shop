Frequently Asked Questions

Why was my payment declined?

Payments can be declined for various reasons including insufficient funds, expired card, incorrect information, or bank security restrictions. Contact your bank to find out the specific reason.

Will I be charged if my payment fails?

No. If your payment was unsuccessful, no charges were applied to your account. Your payment information is secure and encrypted.

How long do I have to retry my payment?

You have 24 hours from the time you won the auction to complete your payment. After 24 hours, your items will be released and made available to other buyers.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit and debit cards processed through PayPal. Ensure your card is active and has sufficient funds.
